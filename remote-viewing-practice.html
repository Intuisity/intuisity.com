Remote Viewing Practice

Practice Remote Viewing With Intuisity

Use simple drawing, hidden image choices, and feedback to explore first impressions and strengthen your intuitive awareness.

Remote viewing practice is a focused way to notice impressions before you see the answer. In Intuisity, you begin by quieting your mind, drawing shapes, colors, textures, or feelings that come to mind, then choosing between hidden target images.

How the Exercise Works

The goal is not to guess perfectly every time. The goal is to capture your first calm impressions before analysis takes over. You draw what you sense, reveal the picture choices, make a selection, and compare your result with the hidden target.

Why Feedback Matters

Remote viewing training becomes more useful when you can compare impressions with real feedback. Over time, you may notice whether you sense shapes, movement, color, mood, texture, or location details more clearly.

Intuisity includes free daily intuition games, remote viewing practice, Treasure Chest friend challenges, astrology insights, positivity prompts, and results tracking.

Keep It Light and Repeatable

Short, repeatable practice builds confidence better than pressure. Treat each session as awareness training: pause, draw, choose, review, and notice what felt clear.
