Friend Intuition Games

Practice first impressions together with an Intuisity Treasure Chest challenge.

How Treasure Chest works

One player arranges five treasure symbols and sends a private challenge link. The invited friend tries to sense or guess the hidden order, locks in the response, and then receives feedback. The sender can see when the challenge was sent, opened, and completed.

What skills does the game exercise?

The game encourages focused attention, visual impressions, pattern recognition, memory, confidence in committing to a choice, and reflection after feedback. Because another person creates the target, the exercise also feels social and less predictable.

Playing with one friend

In a head-to-head challenge, the result can focus on how many tries each player needed. The aim is friendly practice rather than pressure: compare approaches, notice what each person sensed, and try again.

Group challenges

A challenge can include up to five people. When several people compete, completing all games quickly matters, while fewer attempts act as the quality measure. Clear results help the group see who finished fastest with the least trial and error.

Invited players and accounts

An invited friend can open and answer the challenge from the shared link. If that person wants to create a new game and send one back—or challenge someone else—Intuisity asks them to create an account so their games and results can be saved.
