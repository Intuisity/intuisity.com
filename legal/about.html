About Intuisity

A place to practice noticing, trusting, and reflecting on your intuitive signals.

Why Intuisity exists

Intuition can feel mysterious, but practicing it can be simple: pause, notice your first impression, make a choice, and reflect on what happened. Intuisity turns that process into approachable daily activities and games.

What you practice

The activities invite you to notice images, feelings, bodily sensations, patterns, timing, and quiet inner impressions before seeing an answer or outcome. Different modules focus on awareness, inner knowing, pattern recognition, mindfulness, remote viewing practice, and reflective decision-making.

A practice—not a promise

Intuisity provides educational and entertainment experiences for personal reflection. Scores can help you notice patterns in your own practice, but they do not prove paranormal ability and should not be used as a substitute for evidence, professional advice, or careful decision-making.

Made for curious beginners and regular practitioners

You do not need special experience. Begin with a quiet moment, record what you notice before analyzing it, and use the result as feedback. Returning regularly makes it easier to recognize which kinds of impressions feel most natural to you.

About the Author

My name is Kathy Kennedy. I grew up in Colorado and moved to Southern California when I was 18, and I have called it home ever since. I am an enthusiastic animal lover and currently share my home with three dogs and three cats. My only daughter welcomed our first grandson this year, and he already has a little brother on the way.

I created Intuisity as an interactive website and app designed to help people explore their awareness and intuition. My own journey began about eight years ago. Since then, I have listened to mediums, clairvoyants, channelers, and others describe their abilities and the different ways they discovered them.

Although I found their experiences fascinating, I wanted a more direct and practical way to explore these ideas for myself—something people could actively practice instead of only hearing about. That desire became Intuisity: a place where you can try daily intuition games, positivity exercises, remote viewing activities, and personalized astrology tips that offer ideas about where to focus your attention each day.

Videos, books, and other people’s experiences have taught me an enormous amount. But I eventually realized that listening and learning can only take us so far. If we never put what we learn into practice, it is difficult to understand our own strengths and patterns.

I think of intuition practice as exercising a mental muscle. Intuisity is meant to help you practice in a different way, receive feedback, and notice how your own impressions develop over time. You may discover areas you want to explore more deeply—or simply recognize a natural strength you had never noticed before.

I have always felt a strong desire to share what I am learning, but I also understand that not everyone is interested in ideas that feel new or outside the ordinary. I created Intuisity for curious, like-minded people who want practical tools they can add to their personal toolbox.

Social media can sometimes feel filled with more advertising than genuine connection. I believe many people are looking for a positive and engaging way to spend their time, learn about themselves, and connect with others.

Thank you for visiting Intuisity. I am truly delighted that you are here, and I hope you find something meaningful, enjoyable, or useful—even if you simply have fun playing Treasure Chest with your friends.

There is much more to come. I am only getting started!

— Kathy Kennedy
