About Intuisity

A place to practice noticing, trusting, and reflecting on your intuitive signals.

Why Intuisity exists

Intuition can feel mysterious, but practicing it can be simple: pause, notice your first impression, make a choice, and reflect on what happened. Intuisity turns that process into approachable daily activities and games.

What you practice

The activities invite you to notice images, feelings, bodily sensations, patterns, timing, and quiet inner impressions before seeing an answer or outcome. Different modules focus on awareness, inner knowing, pattern recognition, mindfulness, remote viewing practice, and reflective decision-making.

A practice—not a promise

Intuisity provides educational and entertainment experiences for personal reflection. Scores can help you notice patterns in your own practice, but they do not prove paranormal ability and should not be used as a substitute for evidence, professional advice, or careful decision-making.

Made for curious beginners and regular practitioners

You do not need special experience. Begin with a quiet moment, record what you notice before analyzing it, and use the result as feedback. Returning regularly makes it easier to recognize which kinds of impressions feel most natural to you.
