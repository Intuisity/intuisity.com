Intuisity Frequently Asked Questions

Learn how Intuisity helps people practice intuition through daily exercises, reflection, progress tracking, and games with friends.

What is Intuisity?

Intuisity is an interactive intuition-training platform with guided activities for awareness, inner knowing, pattern recognition, mindfulness, remote viewing practice, reflective decision-making, and self-discovery.

How does Intuisity help train intuition?

Intuisity gives users repeatable exercises that encourage them to notice first impressions, subtle patterns, feelings, images, timing, and other intuitive signals before reviewing results and tracking progress.

What activities are available in Intuisity?

Activities include Treasure Chest friend challenges, Train Your Knowing, Positivity Practice, Read the Person, personalized astrology insights, and remote viewing challenges.

Can I try Intuisity before creating an account?

Yes. New visitors can try three games before creating a free account. An account is then required to continue playing, save scores and streaks, track progress, and send friend challenges.

How do Treasure Chest friend challenges work?

A player hides five treasure symbols in an order and invites a friend to guess the arrangement. Invited friends can answer from the shared link without first creating an account. They need an account when they want to create and send their own challenge.

Does Intuisity save my progress?

Users who create an account can save challenge activity, scores, progress, profile preferences, and other information described in the Intuisity Privacy Policy.

Are Intuisity astrology insights medical or professional advice?

No. Intuisity activities and astrology insights are intended for personal growth, education, reflection, and entertainment. They do not replace medical, mental-health, legal, or financial advice.
