Free Intuition Games to Play With Friends

Use Intuisity Treasure Chest challenges to practice inner knowing, share a secret note, and compare playful impressions with a friend.

Intuition can be easier to practice when it feels playful. The Intuisity Treasure Chest game lets you choose a hidden order of five treasures, send the challenge by email, and invite a friend to sense the order before opening the chest.

Why Friend Challenges Help

Friend games add real feedback. Your friend can make a guess, send a response, and explore the rest of Intuisity for free. This makes intuition training social, light, and measurable without needing a complicated setup.

Practice Awareness Together

Treasure Chest supports awareness, synchronicity, mindfulness, inner knowing, and personal growth by asking both people to pause and notice the first calm impression. It is free to play and designed for quick daily practice.
